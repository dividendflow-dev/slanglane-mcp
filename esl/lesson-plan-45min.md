## 45-Minute Lesson Plan (B1–C2 ESL / EFL): Register, Irony & "Reading the Room"

1. **Warm-up (10 min) — Literal vs. Intended Meaning**
   - Write three sentences on the board:
     - *"I’m lowkey cooked before Friday’s exam."*
     - *"That presentation ate."*
     - *"Let’s touch grass after this call."*
   - Ask students what *cook*, *eat*, and *grass* mean literally, then compare with how each expression lands in an informal group chat.

2. **Register Sorting (15 min) — Where Does It Fit?**
   - Give student pairs 6 expressions from [`../data/slanglane-sfw-classroom.csv`](../data/slanglane-sfw-classroom.csv).
   - Have them sort each into: *(A) Group chat with friends*, *(B) Casual spoken English*, and *(C) Avoid in formal client/academic writing*.
   - Open each entry on `https://slanglane.com/terms/{slug}` to compare the **When it fits** and **Mind the context** sections.

3. **Regional Contrast (10 min) — US, UK & Irish Everyday English**
   - Contrast British & Irish everyday words (*craic*, *grand*, *sound*, *knackered*, *gutted*, *gobsmacked*, *skint*, *fiver*) with online/US expressions (*bet*, *no cap*, *delulu*, *mid*).

4. **Interactive Practice (10 min)**
   - Run the 5-question SFW **[Daily Slang Sprint](https://slanglane.com/daily)** or test student sentences in the **[Phrase Decoder](https://slanglane.com/decode)**.
